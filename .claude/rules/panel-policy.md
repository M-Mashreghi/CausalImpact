# Panel & Policy Evaluation Rules

For DiD:

- define timing and comparison cohorts,
- assess pre-treatment support,
- inspect pre-trends without treating “non-significant” as proof,
- use clustered inference where appropriate,
- distinguish static and dynamic estimands.

With staggered adoption and heterogeneous effects, do not default to contaminated TWFE lead/lag coefficients. Use cohort/time or interaction-weighted estimands explicitly.

Synthetic control requires donor-pool and pre-treatment-fit diagnostics.
